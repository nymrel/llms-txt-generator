Questions, answered plainly

    
Before you ask

    

      

        What is llms.txt?
        
A plain-text file at yoursite.com/llms.txt that gives AI assistants a curated map of your site — your key pages, products, and anything else worth knowing, written so a language model can read it in seconds.

      

      

        Do I need an llms.txt file?
        
It's not required, but AI assistants increasingly check for one before they crawl your whole site. A small file that points them straight to your best pages makes it far more likely they find and quote you correctly.

      

      

        How do I install the file on my site?
        
Save the generated text as llms.txt and upload it to your site's root folder — not a subfolder — so it loads at yoursite.com/llms.txt.

      

      

        Is my data safe?
        
Yes. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type here leaves your device — there is no server collecting it.

      

      

        What's included in the Deluxe Kit?
        
A five-file zip built from what you typed above: your llms.txt tightened, a robots.txt allowlist naming 12 AI crawlers, an Organization JSON-LD block, and a printable install checklist written for your platform. It is generated here in your browser and downloads as soon as you're back from checkout.
